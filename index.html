Our Journey

A Century of Growing

Tracing our roots from humble beginnings to a modern industry leader.

1900s

Where It All Began

Otto Muller establishes the family's roots in the greenhouse industry after emigrating from Germany.

1910

A New Chapter in New York

The family operation continues in Whitestone, serving the New York City carriage trade.

1929

Growing the Foundation

The family expands its greenhouse operations and begins building the foundation for the company that would follow.

1960

Kurt Weiss Greenhouses

The operation is incorporated as Kurt Weiss Florist, the predecessor of today's company.

1970s

A New Home

The company relocates to Center Moriches as its customer base evolves toward larger-volume horticultural customers.

1980s

Growing Beyond New York

Expansion moves beyond the region, taking advantage of different growing conditions and expanding the company's reach.

Today

Five Generations Later

Kurt Weiss Greenhouses continues to combine family values, horticultural expertise, innovation, and large-scale growing.
